# Third-party models

Author: Kenney (https://kenney.nl)
Original pack: Graveyard Kit, https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit
License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0).
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Downloaded self-contained GLB conversions from:
https://github.com/Hidencod/tge-assets/tree/main/packs/graveyard-kit

Files: character-skeleton.glb, rocks.glb, column-large.glb,
gravestone-cross-large.glb.
Models are unmodified; runtime placement/scaling and animation selection are
configured in ImportedEnemy.gd and WorldBuilder.gd.

Dark soil texture: generated for this project, no third-party texture dependency.
